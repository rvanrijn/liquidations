Liquidation Magnet Hypothesis

Core Hypothesis
In crypto perpetual futures, the side with greater estimated liquidation USD acts as a price magnet. Price is more likely to move toward the larger liquidation cluster first, triggering cascading liquidations before reversing.

If Long liquidations ($3.6B) > Short liquidations ($1.9B) → price drops to sweep longs first.

How It Works

Snapshot imbalance → Track price direction → Resolve at ±0.5% → Log & repeat

StepDetail
1. SnapshotWhen long/short liq USD imbalance exceeds 1.1x, record BTC price + both sides' totals
2. TrackPoll every 10s. If imbalance flips sides, silently reset (no battle logged)
3. ResolveWhen price moves ±0.5% from snapshot: record which direction moved first
4. ScoreIf price moved toward the bigger side → hypothesis correct. Persist to SQLite

Signal Engine

Post-sweep reversal signals built on top of the magnet monitor:

GateCondition
BiasMagnet imbalance ≥ 1.5x
Sweep5m candle enters liq zone (±0.4% of magnet price), needs 3/5 criteria: range > 1.2x avg, wick ≥ 40%, close reclaims, level breach, no continuation
ReclaimPost-sweep candle closes back beyond magnet price and holds
Orderflow1-minute BTC delta aligns with signal direction (positive for LONG, negative for SHORT)
RiskStop distance ≤ 0.6% from entry

Signal fires only when all gates pass. Targets: nearest liq levels on the opposite side.
